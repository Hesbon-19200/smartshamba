# MkulimaAI Hackathon 3-Minute Demonstration Script

## Demo Overview
- **Name**: Demo Farmer (Hesbon)
- **Location**: Kisii County, Kenya (Highland fertile zone)
- **Shamba**: 2.0 Acres
- **Question**: *"Should I plant tomatoes on my 2-acre shamba, what are my risks, what profit can I expect, and how do I manage blight?"*

---

## 3-Minute Walkthrough for Hackathon Judges

### Step 1: Instant One-Click Demo Load (30 Seconds)
1. In the header or dashboard banner, click the prominent **"Load Hackathon Demo Scenario"** button.
2. The platform instantaneously loads the Kisii 2-acre farm profile with authentic agro-ecological parameters:
   - Soil: Volcanic Red Loam
   - Water: River Gravity + Drip irrigation
   - Budget: KES 150,000
   - Historical rainfall: High annual precipitation (1,800 mm)

### Step 2: Farm Decision & Profit Prediction (60 Seconds)
1. Review the generated **Farm Analysis**:
   - **Suitability Score**: `91/100` (High compatibility with volcanic soil & water access)
   - **Financial Projection**:
     * Total Input & Labour Costs: `KES 128,000`
     * Projected Yield: `580 crates (64kg)`
     * Estimated Market Price: `KES 3,200 / crate`
     * Gross Revenue: `KES 1,856,000`
     * Net Projected Profit: `KES 1,728,000`
     * Potential ROI: `~1,350%`
   - **Explainable AI Breakdown**:
     * ✓ High soil compatibility for solanaceous crops
     * ✓ Gravity water minimizes drought vulnerability
     * ⚠ Price volatility warning during peak harvest supply
   - **Model Metrics**: Benchmark comparison between `RandomForestRegressor (R² = 0.88)` and `GradientBoostingRegressor`.

### Step 3: Crop Doctor Multimodal Scan (45 Seconds)
1. Navigate to **Crop Doctor**.
2. Select the pre-loaded **"Tomato Early Blight"** sample leaf image.
3. Click **"Analyze Crop Leaf"**.
4. The Vision Engine detects:
   - **Condition**: *Possible Tomato Early Blight (Alternaria solani)* (Confidence: 87.4%)
   - **Severity**: Moderate
   - **Observed Symptoms**: Concentric ring lesions, yellow halo.
   - **Safety Boundary**: Explicitly flags as AI-assisted estimate, recommends non-chemical cultural sanitation, warns against unapproved chemical mixtures, and advises extension officer visit.

### Step 4: Conversational AI Agronomist (45 Seconds)
1. Switch to **AI Agronomist**.
2. Click the sample prompt: *"How do I protect my 2-acre tomato crop in Kisii from early blight during the long rains?"*
3. The Agronomist answers with localized context:
   - Advises mulching with dry grasses to prevent soil-splash onto lower leaves.
   - Suggests 60cm plant spacing for air circulation in humid Kisii highlands.
   - Clarifies proper crop rotation with maize or beans.
   - Recommends local KALRO/sub-county extension officer engagement.
